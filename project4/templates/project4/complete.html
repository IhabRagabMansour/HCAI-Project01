What This Study Was Testing

Both interfaces were fitting the same kind of model of your taste: a weight for each film property — genre, length, era, rating, origin — that together predict which of two films you would pick. What differed was only how you were asked: a series of choices between two films, or a few rankings of ten. The question is whether one of those ways teaches the model more about you per unit of your effort.
