How This Works

You will use two different interfaces for expressing your film preferences. In both of them, please answer based on what you would personally rather watch — not what you think is the better film, or what is more famous.

Choosing between two films

You see two films side by side. Click the one you would rather watch. There is no “correct” answer and you cannot go back, so go with your instinct.

Ranking ten films

You see {{ config.ranking_size }} films. Put them in order from most preferred at the top to least preferred at the bottom, using drag-and-drop or the move buttons.

Before You Start

  • You will do a couple of practice tasks first — these are just to get familiar with the interface and are not analysed.
  • If you do not recognise a film, that is fine — judge it from the title, year, genres, and other details shown.
  • There is a short break between the two interfaces.
  • Please complete the study in one sitting if you can.
