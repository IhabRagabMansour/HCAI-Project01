Participant Information

What is this study about?
We are comparing two ways of learning what kinds of films a person enjoys. In one part you will choose between two films; in another you will put ten films in order of preference. We want to find out which approach captures your taste more efficiently.

What will I be asked to do?
You will answer a few short background questions, complete some practice tasks, then work through both interfaces ({{ config.n_pairwise_trials }} pairwise choices and {{ config.n_ranking_trials }} rankings of {{ config.ranking_size }} films), with a short questionnaire after each and a break in between. At the end there are a few final preference questions. It takes roughly 15–20 minutes.

There are no right or wrong answers.
We are interested only in what you would rather watch.

What data is collected?
Your choices and rankings, which films were shown, how long each task took, and your questionnaire answers. You are identified only by a random code. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.

Can I withdraw?
Yes. You may stop at any point by closing the page, without giving a reason and without any penalty. Data from incomplete sessions is excluded from analysis.

How is my data stored?
Responses are stored under your random participant code, kept securely, and used only for this research. Results are reported in aggregate, so no individual can be identified.
