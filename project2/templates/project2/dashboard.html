Accumulated Local Effects (ALE)

Exact vs. approximate derivatives. Logistic regression is differentiable, so its partial derivatives are exact: ∂pc/∂xj = pc(βcj − ∑r prβrj). A decision tree is piecewise-constant — its derivative is zero almost everywhere and undefined at split thresholds — so ALE must use finite differences (discretization), which is what this implementation does for both models. PDP averages over the marginal distribution (and may probe unrealistic feature combinations); ALE uses local differences within bins of the conditional distribution, avoiding that extrapolation.
