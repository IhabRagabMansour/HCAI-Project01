Deferral Strategies

For each article the system chooses to predict (classifier) or defer (expert). We compare two strategies:

  • Expert-advantage (Bayes-optimal rule): defer when P(expert correct | x) > maxy P(y | x) — i.e. when the expert is more likely correct than the classifier is confident. P(expert correct | x) is learned from TF-IDF text; the classifier's softmax confidence estimates its own correctness.
  • Confidence threshold (naïve baseline): defer when the classifier's top probability falls below a tuned threshold (τ = {{ confidence_threshold|floatformat:3 }}). This ignores whether the expert is actually better — the limitation the lecture highlights.
