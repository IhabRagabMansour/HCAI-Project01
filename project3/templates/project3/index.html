Learning to Defer

This project builds a human–AI team for topic classification of news articles. A classifier predicts the topic, but for each article the system may instead defer the decision to a (simulated) human expert. The goal is to learn when deferring is beneficial — and, crucially, to learn this efficiently by actively querying the expert only on the most informative examples, since expert labels are expensive.

  • Task 1 — Baseline classifier: train a text classifier on AG News.
  • Task 2 — Simulated expert: an imperfect expert with region-specific competence.
  • Task 3 — Learning to defer: decide per article whether to predict or defer.
  • Task 4 — Active learning: query the expert efficiently to learn the deferral policy.

Dataset: AG News
